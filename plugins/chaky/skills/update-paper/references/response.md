# Response to reviewers (rebuttal or revision letter; used by `update-paper`)

open with thanks and a 2-3 line summary of the main changes. Group duplicate points; quote each concern briefly, then answer: **changed** (what and where, with page or section) / **clarified** (the misunderstanding and the new text) / **respectfully disagree** (evidence and citation) / **out of scope** (why, and where it is now acknowledged). Never promise what the paper won't contain. Keep within the venue's word limit; most important concerns first.

Each concern is fixed at its root cause (`AGENTS.md`), not just reworded. Every fix becomes a ticket.
