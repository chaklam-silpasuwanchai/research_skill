# Contribution type decides the method (AIT Brain Lab)

Choose the contribution first. An interview study is not weaker than an experiment; it answers a different question.

| Type | Typical question | Specify | Typical evidence and analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Experimental** | Does X affect Y? | IV and levels; DV and operational measure; between, within, or mixed design; task; procedure; sample; randomization or counterbalancing; control variables and likely confounds | Descriptive plots; t-test, ANOVA, regression, or mixed-effects models; effect sizes, confidence intervals, assumption checks |
| **Design research** | What design knowledge can we produce? | Human need; grounding theory; design goals; alternatives; rationale; iterations; what transfers beyond the artifact | Formative interviews; co-design; workshops; diaries; field deployment; think-aloud; thematic analysis |
| **Artifact or system** | What new capability does it provide? | Capability; architecture; intended users and tasks; baseline; success criteria; limits | Accuracy, latency, reliability, resource use; usability and task performance; benchmark comparison; ablation |
| **Exploratory qualitative** | What is happening in this new situation? | Phenomenon and context; why exploration is needed; participant selection; researcher position; protocol | Semi-structured interviews, observation, fieldwork, focus groups; thematic analysis or grounded theory; negative cases; triangulation |
| **Model or dataset** | How well does it work, and where does it fail? | Data source and representativeness; train/validation/test; metrics; strong baselines; reproducibility; leakage and bias | Benchmark comparison; statistical uncertainty; ablations; error analysis; robustness; subgroup analysis; human evaluation |

Check three kinds of validity: **internal** (did X cause Y?), **construct** (did we measure the concept?), **external** (where does it hold?).

Record the type in `experimental_design.md` and `current_status.md`.
