# Slop patterns (scan list)

**Stock phrases:** "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "realm", "crucial / pivotal / vital role", "it is important to note", "it is worth noting", "in today's fast-paced world", "in recent years, X has attracted increasing attention", "plays a key role", "sheds light on", "paves the way", "a testament to", "bridge the gap", "growing body of literature" (no citation), "has gained significant traction".

**Inflated words:** leverage, utilize, harness, showcase, underscore, comprehensive, robust, seamless, holistic, novel (unsupported), significantly (outside statistics).

**Structures:** "not only X but also Y" and "It's not X, it's Y" repeated; padding in threes; every paragraph shaped claim → elaboration → moral; rhetorical-question openers; trailing "-ing" tails ("..., highlighting the importance of ..."); vague attribution ("studies show", "researchers argue"); em-dash overuse; "Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally" chains; a conclusion that only restates; generic limitation or future-work lines ("other datasets could be explored").

**Rhythm:** same sentence length throughout; lists of three adjectives; perfectly symmetric parallelism.

**Fix moves:** delete the filler → state the fact; swap the abstract noun for the real actor and a verb; replace "studies show" with the citation; replace the generic limitation with the specific threat to validity; break one long, balanced sentence into a short one and a long one.
