# What Chaky can do (one line each)

Say "guide me" and Chaky suggests a skill; you choose. You can also name one directly, or type `/<skill> <focus>`.

| Skill | One line | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| `guide-me` | Start here: what needs attention, which skill could help next (you choose), progress, advisor update, retro | "guide me", "what next?", "show my progress" |
| `setup-repo` | Sets up the project: folders, git, LaTeX template, working files, first board | "set up my paper", /setup-repo chi |
| `setup-writing-style` | Learns your writing voice from your past papers | "learn my writing style" |
| `setup-llms` | OpenRouter, local LLM, free AI chats for labeling and outside reviews | "set up OpenRouter", "help me label data" |
| `brain-dump` | Interviews you one question at a time, any phase, and saves what it learns | "interview me", /brain-dump my study |
| `check-board` | Recap; tasks we need, forgot, or can drop; priorities; plain names; auto-close | "check the board", "recap", /check-board |
| `validate-idea` | Is the idea (or the paper so far) novel, valid, significant? How to sharpen it | "is this novel?", /validate-idea <idea> |
| `find-papers` | Closest work, novelty double-check, methods to borrow; watch; a paper you found | "find closest work", "I found a paper" |
| `plan-method` | What you change and measure, participants, sample size, pilots, freeze, runs | "plan my method", /plan-method IV |
| `write` | Writes the paper section by section in your voice; stats, figures, unslop | "write my intro", /write abstract |
| `review` | Rigorous reviewer for your venue: novelty, validity, writing, every citation; a checklist back | "review my paper", /review citations |
| `think-deeply` | 360° look at the latest idea or answer: is there a better solution? | "think deeply", "is there a better way?" |
| `clarify` | Rewrites a too-technical answer in plain words, with an example and what needs you | "clarify", "give me an example" |
| `teach-me` | Teaches a concept or step one small step at a time, on your own project | "teach me", /teach-me power analysis |
| `remember-this` | Makes a repeated instruction permanent; adds a skill | "always do X", "remember this" |
