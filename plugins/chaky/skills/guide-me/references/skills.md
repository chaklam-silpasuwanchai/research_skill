# What Chaky can do (one line each)

Say "guide me" and Chaky suggests a skill; you choose. You can also name one directly.

| Skill | One line | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| `guide-me` | Shows what needs attention, suggests a skill for the next step (you choose), progress, advisor update, retro | "guide me", "what next?", "show my progress" |
| `start-paper` | Interviews you, sets up the repo, learns your voice, seeds the board | "set up my paper" |
| `plan-tickets` | The board: tickets, 1-5 priorities, done-when, assist or lead mode | "show the board", "what should I do first?", "let me lead" |
| `shape-idea` | Turns a rough interest into a publishable question and first draft | "help me find a research question" |
| `tidy-board` | Re-checks priorities, drops or merges tasks you don't need, renames tasks in plain words | "clean up my tickets", "is this task needed?" |
| `grill-me` | Interviews you one question at a time to stress-test an idea, plan, or argument | "grill me" |
| `run-study` | Plans and runs your study: pilots, frozen plan, logged runs, reflection | "plan my experiments" |
| `write-paper` | Writes the paper with you, section by section | "write my introduction" |
| `review-paper` | Tough reviewer (quick or full) and the submission check | "quick review", "can I submit?" |
| `find-papers` | Finds and verifies papers; scoop watch; handles a new paper you found | "find related work", "I found a new paper" |
| `polish-prose` | Clear writing in your voice; removes robotic AI phrasing | "unslop this" |
| `analyze-results` | Statistics and honest figures | "which test?", "make a figure" |
| `write-code` | Tested, reproducible research code | "help me code this reliably" |
| `use-llms` | Model access plan; free-chat labeling and outside reviews | "what model can I use?", "help me label data" |
| `think-deeply` | Challenges the latest message: first principles, hidden assumptions, one thread in depth, knock-on effects | "think deeply", "challenge this" |
| `clarify` | Rewrites a hard-to-read AI answer in plain words: what it means for you, what needs your attention | "clarify", "say that simply" |
| `teach-chaky` | Makes a repeated instruction permanent; adds supporting skills | "always do X", "add a skill" |
