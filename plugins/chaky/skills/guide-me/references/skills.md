# What Chaky can do

Say "guide me" and Chaky suggests a skill; you choose. Or type `/<skill> <focus>`.

| When you... | Use | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| are lost | `guide-me` | Where you are, what needs you, which skill helps now |
| want to know what your tasks are | `update-board` | Recap, then your to-do list fixed and prioritized |
| are confused by what the AI said | `clarify` | Bottom line, what needs you, one example |
| don't know enough to follow what the AI said | `teach-me` | Small steps with a check after each |
| think the AI isn't thinking in enough ways | `think-deeply` | Every angle, 2-3 paths, one recommendation |
| want the AI to get your feedback | `brain-dump` | One question at a time; your answers saved |
| suspect something: has someone done this? is it new? | `read-papers` | 10-15 closest papers: what to copy, your one difference |
| plan to try something | `plan-method` | Fair experiment and tools, learned from 5-15 papers; pilot and run |
| want to write | `update-paper` | Your story first (with alternative storylines), then sections |
| want reviewers' opinions | `review` | Three reviewers, a meta-review, citations, easy and difficult fixes |
| feel the AI doesn't write like you | `setup-writing-style` | Learns from a paragraph, papers, emails, blogs; confirms your terms |
| want the repo tidy and up to date | `update-repo` | Updates all docs, cleans up clutter |
| want the AI to always talk simply, in your language | `speak-plainly` | Plain words, your terms, examples, from now on |
