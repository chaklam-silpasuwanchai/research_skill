# Brain Dump

The researcher knows more than they have written down. Pull it out, one question at a time, and save it. Works in **any phase**. Optional focus: `/brain-dump my study design`, `/brain-dump terms`, `/brain-dump why this matters`.

## Rules
- **One question at a time**, then wait. Never a wall of questions.
- **Offer a suggested answer** with each question ("My guess: X, because Y. Right?"). They accept, change, or skip.
- **Look before you ask.** If `components/` or the paper already answers it, confirm instead of asking.
- **Follow their thread.** Ask the question that unlocks the most; go deeper on what they care about, not through a fixed form.
- Use **their words**; record new terms as they appear. Rough answers are welcome. Kind in tone, persistent on substance.

## What to ask about (pick by phase or focus)
- **Start / idea:** what do you want to find out, and why do you care? Who would use the answer? What do you expect to happen? Any paper that feels close?
- **Terms:** the 3-5 words the idea depends on; one meaning and one example each.
- **Plan:** what you will change and what you will measure; who takes part or which data; what could go wrong; what result would change your mind.
- **Argument (writing):** each claim → its evidence → the strongest counterexample → the honest limitation → why it matters.
- **Stuck or unsure:** what feels off? What did you try? What would you do with one more week?

Every ~4 questions, show "**So far**" in 3-5 bullets.

## Stop when
They can say the idea, plan, or argument in **one sentence**; or they say "enough"; or after about 12 questions (offer to continue another time).

## Save (just do it, then say what was saved)
Update `terms.md`, `ideas.md`, `experimental_design.md`, `current_status.md`, or the board as fits; log decisions and dropped ideas in `log.md` (their answers → `user`; your unconfirmed guesses → `ai-suggested`). Give the one-sentence version, one line of what was saved, and suggest a next skill (`validate-idea`, `plan-method`, `write`, `check-board`).
