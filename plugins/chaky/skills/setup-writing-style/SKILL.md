# Setup Writing Style

1. **Get samples.** Past papers (paths or links; they stay outside the repo), or 2-3 paragraphs they wrote. Co-authored → ask which parts are theirs.
2. **Note their habits** in `components/voice.md` (about 20 lines), each with a short quote: sentence length, "we" and tense, favorite signal words, hedging ("suggests" vs "shows"), how they state the gap, what to **keep**, and slips they want fixed.
3. **Show** the notes and one paragraph rewritten in their voice. Save, and say "tell me anything that doesn't sound like you."
4. **Refresh** when they edit drafts heavily: their edits show their style.
