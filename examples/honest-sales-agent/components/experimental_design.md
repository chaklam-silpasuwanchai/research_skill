# Experimental Design (CHI style)

## Research questions and hypotheses
| ID | Research question | Hypothesis | Would be challenged by |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | Does proactive disclosure change perceived trust in a sales agent? | H1: proactive disclosure raises perceived trust vs no disclosure | No difference, or lower trust, with adequate power |
| RQ2 | Does it change purchase intention? | H2: purchase intention drops slightly but stays above the no-trust baseline | Large drop, or no change |

## Independent variables
- Disclosure: none / proactive / on request (between participants)

## Dependent variables
- Perceived trust ([validated trust scale: TO DECIDE]); purchase intention (1-7); claim-accuracy audit of the agent's transcripts

## Task and procedure
