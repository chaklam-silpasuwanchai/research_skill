# My writing voice (example notes)

- **Sentence length and rhythm:** mixes short claims with one long explanatory sentence
- **Voice and tense:** "we" + active; present tense for claims, past for procedures
- **Signal words I like:** "In contrast," "Concretely,"
- **Paragraph habits:** claim first, then evidence, then one-line takeaway
- **Hedging style:** direct; hedges only when data are mixed
- **Recurring English slips to fix (asked):** missing articles ("the"), plural "data" verb agreement
- **Keep:** concrete examples early
- **Change:** long stacked "which" clauses
